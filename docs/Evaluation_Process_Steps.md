# Evaluation Process Steps

## Step 1: Log in as Tester

1. Open the app at http://localhost:5173/.
2. On the login screen, choose **Tester** and sign in with the demo account it shows.
3. On the Tester bar, find **Demo pair**. Call the first professor **Professor A** (the observee) and the second **Professor B** (the observer).

## Step 2: AC prepares the cycle

1. On the Tester bar, set **Dallas date/time** to **Sep 1, 2026 10:00 AM** and click **Set date**.
2. Set **View** to **Assessment Committee**.
3. Open **Settings → Cycle & Deadlines** and check the Fall 2026 deadlines.
4. Open **Evaluation Criteria** and check the template.
5. Open **Dashboard** and click the **Not signed up** filter in **Outstanding & Upcoming Evaluations**.

## Step 3: Both professors sign up to be observed

1. Set **View** to **Faculty (observee / observer)**.
2. Under **Demo pair**, click **Look as** Professor A.
3. Open **Evaluations** and click **Sign Up to Be Observed**.
4. Under **1. Class**, pick the class.
5. Under **2. Days**, pick one or more days.
6. Click **Sign up**.
7. Click **Look as** Professor B and repeat steps 3–6.

## Step 4: AC runs observer selection

1. Set the date to **Sep 16, 2026** and click **Set date**.
2. Set **View** to **Assessment Committee** and open **Dashboard**.
3. Click **Run Observer Selection**.
4. In the **Observer Selection** panel, check that Professor A's signup lists Professor B.
5. Click **Notify all observees**.

## Step 5: Professor A sends a request

1. Set **View** to **Faculty (observee / observer)** and click **Look as** Professor A.
2. Open **Evaluations**.
3. In **Request observers**, tick Professor B.
4. Under **Proposed dates**, check the dates, or click **Add a proposed date**.
5. Click **Send request**.

## Step 6: Professor B confirms

Do this within 48 hours of Step 5 on the app clock.

1. Click **Look as** Professor B and open **Evaluations**.
2. Under **Needs your attention**, click **View requests**.
3. Click **Confirm** on Professor A's request.

## Step 7: Professor A chooses the observer

1. Click **Look as** Professor A and open **Evaluations**.
2. Under **Choose your observer**, pick the date next to Professor B, if more than one is offered.
3. Click **Choose**.

## Step 8: Professor B fills in and signs the report

1. Set the date to the observation date, or later, and click **Set date**.
2. Click **Look as** Professor B and open **Evaluations**.
3. Under **Needs your attention**, click **Open report**.
4. Rate every required criterion. Use **N/A** for anything not observed.
5. Add a comment to every criterion rated 1 or 2.
6. Write the **Summary**.
7. Tick the signature box and click **Save and sign**.

## Step 9: Professor A reviews and signs the report

1. Click **Look as** Professor A and open **Evaluations**.
2. Under **Needs your attention**, click **Review and sign**.
3. Optionally, add comments and click **Save comments**.
4. Tick the signature box and click **Sign report**.

If the observation didn't happen, either professor can click **Report that it didn’t happen** on the report, fill in **What happened?**, and click **Submit report** instead of Steps 8–9.

## Step 10: AC sends the survey notice

1. Set the date to **Nov 16, 2026** and click **Set date**.
2. Set **View** to **Assessment Committee** and open **Dashboard**.
3. Click **Send survey notice**.

## Step 11: Both professors take the survey

1. Set **View** to **Faculty (observee / observer)** and click **Look as** Professor A.
2. Open **Evaluations** and click **Take survey**.
3. Answer **Was this process OK?**, **Difficulties you had** and **How could the process improve?**.
4. Click **Submit survey**.
5. Click **Look as** Professor B and repeat steps 2–4.

## Step 12: AC reviews the results

1. Set the date to **Dec 16, 2026** and click **Set date**.
2. Set **View** to **Assessment Committee**.
3. Open **Dashboard** and read the KPI cards, **By Hire Level** and **Unmatched Faculty**.
4. Open **Reports** and check that the report shows **Signed off**.
5. Open **Evaluations** to see the cycle statistics.
6. Open **Faculty** and check that Professor A's next evaluation moved forward.
7. Set the date back to today and click **Set date**.
